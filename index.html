Berghia and Aiptasia, Answered

What are Berghia nudibranchs?

Berghia are small, reef-safe sea slugs that eat aiptasia and nothing else. They're the natural, chemical-free way to clear an aiptasia infestation from a reef aquarium.

Do Berghia really get rid of aiptasia?

Yes. Berghia are obligate aiptasia predators, meaning aiptasia is their only food. Given enough time and the right numbers for your tank, they hunt down aiptasia you can't even see, including in rockwork you can't reach.
